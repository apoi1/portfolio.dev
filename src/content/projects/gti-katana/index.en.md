The internal productivity tool I built for **Sutherland's Global Technology Infrastructure team** while supporting international campaigns on-site. The team needed a lightweight way to organize simple day-to-day activities — so I designed and shipped **GTI Katana** on my own initiative, as the internal answer to "how do we stop losing track of the small stuff".

## What it is

A single-page app the team opens to organize and track its activities — the screenshots on this page are from the running app in production, not mockups.

- **React 19** SPA on Vite — small bundle, instant loads, deployed on Vercel.
- **Tailwind CSS** for the interface, **Firebase** for data.
- Hosted publicly for the team at [tilinapp.vercel.app](https://tilinapp.vercel.app) — a deliberate zero-ops choice: no servers to babysit while everyone's busy keeping campaigns alive.

## Why it matters as a story

This wasn't an assigned ticket — it was a gap I saw while doing on-site support (~7 tickets a day) and filled. The whole path is there: identify the friction, design a small tool people actually want to open, ship it where the team can reach it, and keep it running. Sometimes the best infra work looks deceptively simple.
